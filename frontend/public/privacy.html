Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

InstantInterviewPrep builds a personal interview prep kit from your resume, a job description and the company you are interviewing with. This page explains what we store, why, and who else sees it.

What we store

  • Your account: first and last name and email address. If you use a password, we store only a one-way hash of it (Argon2id), never the password itself. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and nothing else from your Google account.
  • Your prep kits: the resume, job description, notes and files you provide, the content generated for you, and your progress (revised topics, practice answers and quiz scores).
  • Sign-in sessions: a random session ID kept in a cookie, plus the browser and IP address that started each session, used for security.

How it is used

Only to provide the service: to generate and show your prep kits, keep you signed in, send password-reset emails, and protect accounts from abuse (for example, limiting repeated sign-in attempts). We do not sell your data, show ads or use tracking or analytics cookies.

Who else processes your data

  • Anthropic (when AI mode is on): your resume, job description and notes are sent to Anthropic's API to research the company and write your kit.
  • Google: if you choose Continue with Google, to confirm who you are.
  • Our email provider: to deliver password-reset emails to your address.

Cookies

One essential cookie keeps you signed in, and a short-lived cookie protects the Google sign-in step. Both are needed for the site to work; there are no other cookies.

Your choices

Your prep kits are visible only to your account. You can delete any kit at any time from My prep kits. To delete your account and everything in it, reply to any email you received from InstantInterviewPrep (such as a password-reset email) and ask for deletion; we will remove it.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS, passwords and session IDs are stored as hashes, and each account can only see its own data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
